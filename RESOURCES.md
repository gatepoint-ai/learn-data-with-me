# Resources

Free courses and official documentation I recommend. Every link was checked when it was added.

## Courses

- [dbt Fundamentals](https://learn.getdbt.com/courses/dbt-fundamentals), dbt Labs: models, sources, tests and documentation, from zero. About five hours.
- [Data Engineering Zoomcamp](https://github.com/DataTalksClub/data-engineering-zoomcamp), DataTalks.Club: a free, hands-on course covering orchestration, warehousing, dbt, batch and streaming.

## Modelling

- [Kimball Group: Dimensional modeling techniques](https://www.kimballgroup.com/data-warehouse-business-intelligence-resources/kimball-techniques/dimensional-modeling-techniques/): grain, facts, dimensions and slowly changing dimensions, from the people who named them.
- [dbt: How we structure our dbt projects](https://docs.getdbt.com/best-practices/how-we-structure/1-guide-overview): staging, intermediate and marts.

## Testing and data quality

- [dbt: data tests](https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/build/data-tests)
- [dbt: source freshness](https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/build/sources#source-data-freshness)
- [Google SRE book, Data Integrity](https://sre.google/sre-book/data-integrity/)

## Orchestration

- [Apache Airflow: tutorial](https://airflow.apache.org/docs/apache-airflow/stable/tutorial/fundamentals.html)
- [Apache Airflow: best practices](https://airflow.apache.org/docs/apache-airflow/stable/best-practices.html)

## Databases and migration

- [PostgreSQL: Backup and Restore](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/backup.html)
- [PostgreSQL: pg_dump](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/app-pgdump.html) and [pg_dumpall](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/app-pg-dumpall.html)
- [DuckDB documentation](https://duckdb.org/docs/): the in-process database the leakage pipeline runs on.
